Built around commercial snow service

The next snow-service build starts with the properties it needs to cover. At Durand’s shop in Aldergrove, the current Ford F-550 builds bring together 12-foot dump boxes, SnowDogg V-plows and commercial salters for industrial snow service and large parking lots.

The photographs show the equipment at the shop: a stainless SnowDogg V-plow at the front of a Ford truck and a Henderson-branded hopper installed in a black dump body.

A plow and salter plan for the properties on the route

For a contractor, the conversation covers more than the blade at the front. The truck, dump body, salter and route need to be considered together, including the property layout, where material will be loaded and how the equipment will be used through a service shift.

For your own build, discuss the plow fitment, available payload and operating limits for the truck and equipment combination you are considering.
