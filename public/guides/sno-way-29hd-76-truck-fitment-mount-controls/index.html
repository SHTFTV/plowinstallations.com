Sno-Way 29HD 7′6″: match the truck, mount and controls

A focused Sno-Way 29HD guide: compare blade width, installed load, mounting generation and optional equipment before an installation enquiry with Durand.

PlowInstallations Editorial · November 2, 2026 · Manufacturer sources checked November 2

The model behind today’s guide

Sno-Way’s current product page identifies the 29HD 7-foot-6-inch straight blade in its Tundra Ram range. It lists a 90-inch blade width, 78-inch plowing width at 30 degrees, a 29-inch blade height and approximately 571 lb without the mount. The Drive In mount is listed alongside optional Down Pressure hydraulics, wireless control and E-Z Switch wings. These are manufacturer specifications, not our measurements or confirmation of a particular truck application. Read the exact product page.

Use the working width to describe the job

Tell the shop about narrow entrances, parked vehicles, turning space and the areas where snow must be moved. Put your route sketch beside the proposed equipment description. A blade’s headline width does not answer every access question. Ask which dimensions and configuration the supplier used when considering the work. This is a planning conversation, not a promise that one plow will clear a particular property.

Start the fitment record with the actual truck

Sno-Way provides a vehicle-matching tool and separate current Series 2, Classic and archived application references. Use the correct route for the actual equipment generation. Record the truck’s year, make, model, configuration and stated ratings, then ask the supplier to confirm the proposed complete package in writing. Keep any unresolved result open; this article has not run or approved your individual application. Start with the official matching resource and its application-reference index.

Ask what the installed-load figure includes

The published plow weight excludes the mount. Ask for an itemised equipment list that identifies truck-side hardware, fitted accessories and the complete proposed load. Have the installer review the applicable vehicle and manufacturer requirements, including axle ratings and ballast. A bare-plow figure is a starting point for that review. Do not use it as a calculator for an unassessed truck or assume that every pickup in a broad model family has the same approval.

A used 29HD needs its own identity check

Before paying for a used setup, collect the model and serial identification, mounting generation, controller, lights and truck-side parts included. Ask for clear photographs and a list of missing or unknown items. Give that record to the shop before assuming a transfer will be straightforward. A seller’s description of a complete plow may mean something different from a complete installation for your vehicle. Ask which parts can be assessed and which require manufacturer-specific support.

Separate the options from the quoted package

Ask the seller to mark which hydraulic, controller and wing options are actually fitted, and which are only being discussed. Keep the answer with the quotation and the applicable equipment documentation. We have not tested productivity, scraping performance or reliability and make no time-saving promise. For a used machine, ask how the shop will establish the installed configuration and service needs rather than relying on the appearance of a controller or blade.

Give Durand a brief that can become a useful quote

For a Lower Mainland enquiry, send Durand at Full Flow Hydraulics the truck record, exact plow identity, photographs and the work you want assessed. Ask about mounting, electrical compatibility, condition assessment, parts sourcing and the scope of commissioning. Request a written breakdown of equipment, labour, exclusions and unresolved assumptions. Confirm timing directly with the shop; an enquiry is not a booking. This guide does not establish Sno-Way dealer status, stock or approval for any particular installation.

Keep the support record with the equipment

Once work is agreed, keep the final equipment identification, relevant manufacturer documents and service history together. For a fault enquiry, describe what you observed and any known changes without turning an online symptom list into a diagnosis. The preseason repair checklist helps organise the conversation. The installation-cost guide explains quote categories; it is not a price for this 29HD installation.
