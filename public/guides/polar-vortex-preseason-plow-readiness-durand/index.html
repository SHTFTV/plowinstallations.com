Polar-vortex headlines: prepare your plow enquiry with Durand

A practical preseason equipment review for Fraser Valley owners: identify the setup, describe symptoms and confirm service scope with Full Flow Hydraulics.

PlowInstallations editorial · October 28, 2026

Use the news as a prompt to prepare

CTV News reported on October 28 that meteorologists are watching a possible early weakening of the polar vortex, with the outcome for Canada unresolved. That is a reason to review winter readiness, not a prediction of a particular storm. For an equipment owner, the useful response is a clearer service plan. The atmosphere does not diagnose a plow, establish vehicle fitment or tell you which part to replace.

Start with identification, not a parts guess

Before contacting Durand at Full Flow Hydraulics, gather the machine or truck year, make and model, plow make and model, serial information and clear photos of the installed setup. Identify the controller and any existing documentation. Keep the information legible; do not assume an attachment’s brand alone identifies the complete electrical and mounting system.

PREPARE THE ENQUIRYIdentify the setup before selecting parts.

Describe the symptom and when it happens

Explain what the equipment does, what changed and whether the problem occurs cold, warm, under load or intermittently. State any warning, leak or unusual sound you have observed. These observations help define an enquiry; they do not establish the cause. Stop using equipment that appears unsafe and ask a qualified technician about the next step. Do not reach into moving equipment or attempt pressurised hydraulic work from an article.

Separate installation, inspection and repair

A truck-side installation, a used-plow transfer, a preseason inspection and a repair are different scopes. Ask which work is being proposed and what must be checked before parts can be selected. Use the manufacturer’s current documentation for the exact equipment. This article provides an enquiry framework, not a fitment approval, troubleshooting procedure or promise that Full Flow can accept every setup.

Make the regional handoff practical

Owners in Langley, Aldergrove, Abbotsford and Greater Vancouver can discuss the equipment with Durand at the existing Full Flow shop: 26020 62 Ave, Aldergrove, BC. Confirm the requested work, timing, parts situation and charges directly. No extra branches, current stock, fixed service price or authorised manufacturer relationship is established by this article.

Ask about pickup with the actual machine in mind

Ask Durand about equipment pickup options from your location; confirm machine size, loading/access, transport arrangements, charges and availability before booking. A truck, attachment and other machine can require different arrangements. Do not assume free pickup, universal coverage or a promised collection date.

Keep the weather decision separate

Use current local forecasts and warnings when deciding how an approaching event affects operations. Equipment preparation can proceed before the forecast becomes specific. This is PlowInstallations editorial guidance for an enquiry with Durand; it contains no invented quote, personal forecast or claim of work already performed by Full Flow.
